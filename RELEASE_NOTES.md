# v1.1.0 — Strategy financing under deep uncertainty

Frozen RDM model, inputs, and retained results supporting the working paper by Murray A. Rudd. The release includes the original study, the fresh component comparison, and all nine prescribed sampling replications through three samples of 10,000 futures.

Advance common funding and broader sale permission improve different parts of the financing process. The principal funded policy effects meet the final sampling tolerances; two first-region PRIM membership comparisons remain below their required overlap threshold. The evidence retains those failures.

Download the repository source plus both attached result ZIPs. Follow `docs/REPRODUCING.md`; the unpacker verifies the checksums and restores the original output paths. The model and retained numerical evidence are unchanged from the frozen study. This packaging adds portable manuscript plotting scripts, current figure/table correspondence, and installation documentation.

Reproducibility note: the original exact replay checks are retained. A separate portable verifier checks numerical agreement and retained full-ledger replays, and reports regenerated configuration hash differences explicitly. Numerical round-off prevented exact regenerated hashes in the packaging environment; see `docs/REPRODUCING.md`.

The earlier feasibility repository is a pinned external dependency. Original project software is available under the MIT License; see `LICENSE_SCOPE.md` for coverage of this frozen release and earlier revisions. Source-specific data terms remain in force. The manuscript DOI and public link will be added after posting.
